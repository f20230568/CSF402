Electric Board Map (metro map → circuit board)

A compact, circuit-board-like drawing of a gendered forest, built on the Family Metro Map of Korst, Pronk & van Wijk (2020):

  1. Grid & octilinear traces. Vertices are pads on grid points; edges are traces of unit steps in 8 directions (multiples of 45°).
  2. Gender rule (from the metro map).
    Female: last segment horizontal / vertical
    Male: last segment diagonal
    Bends may have unequal lengths: straight (a), one 45° bend (a, b), or a dogleg (a, c, b).
  3. Routing. Each tree grows from its centre (or the Root Node), generation by generation, largest sub-tree first. Every candidate trace is scored:
    cost = length + 0.7·bends + compactness·Δ(box perimeter) − line continuation + look-ahead
    Traces may run on adjacent grid lines (dense parallel buses) but never touch pads or other traces, and never cross.
  4. Forest packing. Trees are laid out separately and packed in rows (largest first) towards the chosen board aspect ratio.
  5. Verification. The finished board is checked independently: pad collisions and clearance, traces through pads, overlapping or crossing traces, and the gender rule for every arrival.

A pad has at most 8 directions, so a vertex with more than 8 edges cannot be drawn without conflicts (they are reported).
