Korst, Pronk & van Wijk (2020)

A schematic tree layout inspired by the London Underground map:

1. Tree Verification

The input graph must be an undirected tree (|E| = |V| - 1 and connected) with no cycles.

2. Gender & Line Angles
Female: Horizontal / Vertical (even × π/4)
Male: Diagonal (odd × π/4)

Transitions between same-gender nodes are straight; different genders use 45° bends.

3. Generation Scaling
Distance = d × αg

Successive generations g shrink by factor α ≤ 1 to prevent subtree overlap.
